# Tete capstone demo script

Target length: 4–6 minutes.

1. **Frame the need.** “Tete helps health-science students practise a repeatable ECG reading process. It is for education only, not clinical diagnosis.” Show the Observe → Reason → Explain → Receive guidance → Revise → Improve cycle.
2. **Invite engagement.** Open the library and point out that diagnoses are hidden behind neutral case labels. Filter by a skill such as rate, then open a case and identify the schematic label, leads, calibration, and paper speed.
3. **Show guided learning.** Choose Guided Practice, select “Not sure” for one item, open a progressive hint, revise the answer, add a brief interpretation, and submit. Emphasize that structured answers alone determine the weighted score and free text is not evaluated.
4. **Connect feedback to action.** Read one deterministic explanation and the recommended next practice area. Open the offline source summary and point to the external attributed resource, labelled as internet-required. Describe the recommendation as practice guidance, not proof of mastery.
5. **Explain progress honestly.** Show the dashboard’s current prototype rating, rated and assisted counts, score history, and skills needing practice. Explain that only the first unseen Rated Assessment per case can change the provisional rating; guided and repeated results stay distinguishable and cannot inflate it.
6. **Demonstrate resilience.** Show the installed/offline status, disconnect after an online load, reload, and open a case. Note that case content and progress remain local, while external resources require a connection.
7. **Close with limitations.** Tete has six synthetic introductory cases across three sinus-rate categories. It has no accounts, cross-device sync, uploads, patient data, live AI, validated psychometrics, or clinical decision support. Browser data clearing can erase progress. Content has automated consistency checks, but expert approval is **Not yet reviewed** and formative learner testing has not yet produced results.
